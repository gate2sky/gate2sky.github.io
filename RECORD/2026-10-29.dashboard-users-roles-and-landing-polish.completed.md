# Dashboard users and roles, landing polish

Completed in gate2sky/gate2sky.github.io#10 (branch `claude/funny-wright-73yeqs`),
a follow-up to #9.

## Landing (`scripts/landing`, regenerated into `pages/index.html`)

- Night sky: still stars, each twinkling on its own clock (3–9 s) and to its
  own brightness (`--peak`), over drifting color glows (`.sky::before/::after`,
  `drift-a`/`drift-b`, tokens `--glow-1..4` per theme). Shooting stars kept.
  Light theme: only the glows.
- SSH terminal: title-bar tabs for the default key and `-i ~/.ssh/gate2sky`,
  each with its own command, copy button and session; a footer per tab
  (`.tfoot`, background `--note`: faint yellow by day, whitish by night).
- Install ("Pick your launchpad"): the One line / Docker / Kubernetes + Traefik
  pills, each opening a `.term.win` window whose tabs are its variants
  (`input.v1`/`input.v2`): curl / wget, and helm install / Traefik port
  (`ports:` block for Traefik's own chart). Docker has no variants.
- Header logo inlined without its dark tile, 40 px (`hero.brand()` strips the
  64×64 rect; the favicon and dashboard logo keep it).

## Installer (`pages/setup.sh`)

- Downloads with curl, or wget when curl is missing (`fetch`, `redirect`),
  including resolving the `latest` tag. `scripts/setup-check.sh` covers the
  curl path; the wget path was tried by hand with a `PATH` without curl.

## Users and roles

- Migration 2 adds `users.added` and `users.role` (`user`/`admin`/`owner`).
- Added users (dashboard, admin socket, wizard) may sign in whatever
  `auth.allowed_*` says (`config.UserAllowed`).
- Roles (`config.UserRole`): `auth.admins` are always owners. Admins manage
  every subdomain and account except owners' and can't make owners; owners
  manage everything; nobody changes their own role, disables or deletes
  themselves. Enforced in `manage.SetUserRole` and `web.mayManageOwner`.
- `DELETE /api/admin/users/{id}` removes the account with its subdomains, keys,
  passkeys and sessions, and closes its tunnels.
- Covered by `TestAddedUsersAndRoles` (e2e).

## Dashboard (`internal/web/ui`)

- Admin: tabs show Users or Subdomains, with counts (and online count).
- Users list: role in a customizable `<select>` (`appearance: base-select`,
  `<selectedcontent>`, icon per option); Enabled as a switch (green, knob
  right / red, knob left); delete behind a `<dialog>`. Rows you can't change
  show the plain value (role with its icon, a light for Enabled).
- Sign out moved to /account, behind a confirmation `<dialog>`
  (`.dialog`, near the top of the page).
- Subdomain page: the hostname heading is the link; the list page is titled
  "Subdomains"; more room under the footer.
